# Glossary

The terms Sparkles coins, or uses in a narrower sense than usual. Shared
vocabulary — the kind a capable engineer from outside the project already
knows — is not repeated here; pages link it to its own authority instead.

Each entry is defined once, in `docs/.vitepress/glossary.json`, and every page that uses a term links to its anchor here. A library's own
terms are also listed in the Terminology section of its specification. The
rules for adding an entry are in
[Writing Specification Prose](./guidelines/spec-prose.md#link-terms-to-the-glossary).
