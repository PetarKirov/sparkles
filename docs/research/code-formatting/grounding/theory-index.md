## Discrepancies & caveats

- **D-TI1 (row 2) — the tree's most-reused quote rests on image-only evidence.** The Hughes
  Remark is quoted or referenced from `theory/index.md`, `theory/combinators.md`,
  `theory/optimality.md`, `theory/layout-preserving.md`, and will be from `concepts.md`, the
  umbrella, and `dmd-lsp-baseline.md`. Its only source is a 130 dpi rendering of p. 3 of a PDF
  with no text layer (see [`theory-combinators`](./theory-combinators.md) D-C1). It is
  independently corroborated in substance by
  [Yelland's footnote](./theory-optimality.md) — different words, same distinction — which is
  the strongest mitigation available. **A second reader should re-render and re-read that page
  before the tree merges.**
- **D-TI2 (row 3) — resolved, and the corrected finding is stronger.** The earlier draft claimed
  "of the ten papers … exactly one mentions comments", on the strength of three greps. All seven
  locally-held extractions have now been counted (row 3) and the two non-zero cases inspected.
  The result differs from the draft in both directions: **APEP does engage substantively** (two
  primitives, row 3a — the draft missed this), while **Wadler's and Swierstra & Chitil's single
  hits are acknowledgements**, not code comments. `theory/index.md` and
  `theory/layout-preserving.md` were both rewritten to the verified counts, and
  `layout-preserving.md` now carries the full table. Hughes remains ungreppable (image-only) and
  is excluded from the count rather than assumed.
- **D-TI3 (rows 22, 23) — two milestone years are unsourceable from this evidence base.** Both
  `llvm-project` and `dart_style` are pinned as depth-1 clones, chosen for disk economy during
  acquisition; the cost is that neither can date its own history. Both rows are marked `*` in
  the doc with a footnote saying exactly this. To upgrade, refetch either repo with history, or
  cite a dated release-notes page.
- **D-TI4 (row 19) — a real year ambiguity, used inconsistently.** Podkopaev & Boulytchev is
  **PSI 2014**, published in LNCS 8974 in **2015**. This tree uses "2015" throughout (following
  APEP's citation style). Harmless, but a reader checking the conference proceedings will find 2014. Worth one parenthetical in `optimality.md`'s Sources.
- **D-TI5 (row 9) — resolved.** The invented "linear in the patch" complexity has been replaced
  with `—`. If a figure is ever wanted there, `dejonge11` §4 must be read first.
- **D-TI6 (rows 24, 25) — bibliographic folklore in the footnote.** The Wadler 1998/2003 dating
  and the Swierstra & Chitil 2004-TR alias are both true as far as this survey knows and both
  unsourced from a local artifact. They are in a `<sub>` caveat footnote, which is the right
  place for them, but they are not evidence.
