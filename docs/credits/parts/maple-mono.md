hue's own face: the regular, bold, italic and bold-italic Maple Mono with Nerd
Font icons and CJK glyphs, built from source with its OpenType features frozen
(`nix/packages/maple-mono`). hue for Android bundles it; the terminal leaves it
out, since the CJK glyphs are most of its size.
